# OpenFan Linux v0.4.2

November 14, 2026

OpenFan now controls the ASUS Pro WS WRX90E-SAGE SE's motherboard fans through its
BMC using local IPMI. The Home and About pages both display version **0.4.2** from
the built application, and backend labels identify active BMC support.

## Fan controls

- Seven named zones: CPU_FAN/CPU_OPT together, CHA_FAN1–5, and W_PUMP+.
- Measured RPM paired with each zone, plus requested duty while OpenFan controls it.
- Original curves restored on release, client disconnect or a stalled-client timeout.
  A saved recovery journal restores curves after a helper crash.
- Exclusive ownership prevents two OpenFan sessions from changing the same zone.
- CPU/chassis targets retain a 20% floor and full speed at the firmware temperature
  source's 70°C point. W_PUMP+ remains at 100%.
- On this board, BMC cards replace NCT6798 PWM cards to avoid conflicting control paths.
  Existing NCT temperature and RPM monitoring remains available.

Support is restricted to the verified ASUS board, BMC product 0x1193, firmware 2.01
with auxiliary revision `22 00 00 00`, and the observed channel/table layouts.
Other firmware layouts fail closed. Only CHA_FAN1 has received a physical speed-change
test; other zone mappings come from live firmware labels and SDR records.
See [BMC implementation and limitations](../wrx90-bmc-fans.md).

## Upgrade

Download `openfan_0.4.2_amd64.deb` and `SHA256SUMS` from the
[v0.4.2 release](https://github.com/russellmm/openfan-linux/releases/tag/v0.4.2).
Close OpenFan, verify the checksum, then install the package. It updates the app and
root helper together; the BMC backend also requires `ipmitool` and a local IPMI device.

Profiles, calibrations, sensor preferences and CPU/GPU power settings are preserved.
Assign curves to the new BMC cards: old NCT channel IDs are not automatically migrated
to different hardware. Other supported motherboard and NVIDIA backends remain available.
See the [installation guide](../installation.md).

## Validation

The full suite contains **297 passing tests**, including 16 BMC cases covering read-only
probing, identity validation, writes/readback, restoration, recovery, ownership and leases.
Live CHA_FAN1 control increased speed from approximately 2,040 to 2,760 RPM at a 70%
target; all seven original curves matched afterward. Physical disconnect, stalled-client
and forced-helper-crash recovery checks also passed. The packaged GUI was checked with
isolated monitor-only settings and real RPM readings.
